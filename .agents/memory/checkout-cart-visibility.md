---
name: Checkout cart visibility
description: Product visibility requirement for mobile checkout layout decisions
---

Show cart products directly on mobile checkout; do not hide them behind a collapsed order-summary control.

**Why:** The user objected when products required opening a dropdown to be seen in checkout. Shortening the page is less important than confirming what is being purchased at a glance.

**How to apply:** When changing the checkout layout, keep product image/name, quantity, and total visible without interaction, while making the form easy to reach below.
